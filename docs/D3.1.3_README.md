# D3.1.3 — Projected runoff (KiN2025) for the Evanger sub-basins

RenewHydro (FME), Work Package 3, activities A3.1.3 / A3.1.5, deliverable D3.1.3.
Prepared by NORCE Norwegian Research Centre (Lu Li).

This package holds **daily projected runoff** for the 21 local sub-catchments of the Evanger hydropower
system (Vestland), and for their area-weighted total (`EVANGER_TOTAL`, 188 km²), from the
**Klima i Norge 2025 (KiN2025)** hydrological projections (NVE / MET Norway / NCCS, 1 km grid).

## Contents

| Path | What |
|---|---|
| `daily_csv/<basin>.csv.gz` | One file per basin, all scenarios and members. Columns: `date`, `scenario`, `model`, `method`, `member`, `runoff_mm_day`, `discharge_m3s` |
| `netcdf/evanger_kin2025_runoff_<scenario>.nc` | One file per scenario: `runoff(basin, member, time)` in mm/day, plus `area_km2(basin)` |
| `basins/evanger_local_subcatchments_utm33.gpkg` | Basin outlines (EPSG:25833), NVE Nedbørfelt exports |
| `basins/evanger_local_subcatchments_wgs84.geojson` | The same outlines in WGS84 |
| `basins/basin_attributes.csv` | Area, NVE runoff normals (`QN6190_mm`, `QN9120_mm`), `vassdragNr`, KiN2025 hist 1991–2020 mean |
| `members.csv` | Every scenario × model × bias-adjustment member, with GCM, RCM, CMIP generation and years |
| `checksums.md5` | MD5 checksums (`md5sum -c checksums.md5`) |

CSV files are gzip-compressed; pandas reads them directly (`pd.read_csv("daily_csv/10_groendalsvatn.csv.gz")`).

## Scenarios and members

| Scenario | Years | Driving models | Members |
|---|---|---|---|
| `hist` | 1971–2020 | 10 CMIP5 + 10 CMIP6 GCM-RCMs | 40 (20 models × 2 bias adjustments) |
| `rcp26` | 2021–2100 | 10 CMIP5 GCM-RCMs | 20 |
| `rcp45` | 2021–2100 | 10 CMIP5 GCM-RCMs | 20 |
| `ssp370` | 2021–2100 | 10 CMIP6 GCM-RCMs | 20 |

- Bias adjustment (`method`): `eqm` and `3dbc-eqm`. A member is `<model>_<method>`.
- HadGEM-driven `rcp26`/`rcp45` runs end in **2098**; `mpi-r1i1p1f1-icon`, `miroc-r1i1p1f1-icon` and
  `ecearthveg-r1i1p1f1-cclm` (`ssp370`) end in **2099**. Missing years are absent (CSV) or NaN (NetCDF).
- The 20 members per scenario are 10 GCM-RCMs × 2 bias adjustments, **not 20 independent** realisations.

## Variables and units

- `runoff_mm_day`: basin-mean daily runoff, mm/day. Source variable `mrro` (kg m⁻² s⁻¹) × 86 400.
  Basin means use coverage weights of the 1 km cells inside each basin outline.
- `discharge_m3s`: `runoff_mm_day × area_km2 × 1000 / 86 400`, with NVE's `areal_km2`.
- `EVANGER_TOTAL`: area-weighted mean of the 21 sub-basins (so its `discharge_m3s` is the sum).

## How to use the data — important

1. **Natural, local runoff.** `mrro` is runoff generated in each 1 km cell by distHBV. It contains no
   regulation, transfers, reservoirs or river routing. Daily peak timing is approximate for larger basins.
2. **Use changes, not absolute values.** For 1991–2020, KiN2025 `hist` runoff is a median **0.80 ×** NVE's
   `QN9120` normals for these basins (range 0.73–0.89, `basin_attributes.csv`). Compute each future member's
   change against **the same member** in `hist` 1991–2020 (delta change), then apply the change to observed or
   NVE-based inflow.
3. **Small basins.** Several sub-basins are 1–2 km², i.e. one or two grid cells.
4. **Different ensembles.** `rcp26`/`rcp45` (CMIP5) and `ssp370` (CMIP6) use different models; differences
   between them are not only due to emissions.

## Method (provenance)

Code: GitHub `luli420/RenewHydro`, branch `claude/evanger-kin2025`.

1. `download_mrro_full_archive.sh` — all 6,778 daily `mrro` files from thredds.met.no (fileServer);
   every file read in full and damaged files downloaded again (`verify_kin2025_archive.py`).
2. `merge_catchment_shapefiles.py` — the 21 local Evanger sub-catchments (the larger `-nevina` upstream
   variants of 03, 12 and 15 excluded) into one GeoPackage.
3. `extract_basin_runoff.py` — coverage-weighted basin means for every member and day.
4. `split_runoff_csv.py` — per-basin files and the area-weighted `EVANGER_TOTAL`.
5. `package_d313.py` — this package.

Analysis of changes in inflow magnitude and seasonality (A3.1.5): `notebooks/evanger_runoff_projections.ipynb`
and the accompanying report.
